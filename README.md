# Eolian

> A modern workflow platform built around relationships, not pipelines.

Eolian is a modern workflow platform that helps teams organize, connect, and evolve their processes without being constrained by rigid CRM structures.

Instead of forcing every business into predefined pipelines, Eolian is designed around customizable workflows, allowing teams to model their own operational processes while keeping data connected and easy to understand.

## Vision

Most CRM platforms optimize for sales.

Eolian optimizes for workflows.

Whether you're managing customers, legal cases, internal operations, support requests, or entirely custom processes, Eolian provides the flexibility to build the workflow your team actually needs.

## Core Principles

* **Workflow-first** — Processes are the center of the platform.
* **Flexible by design** — Adapt the system to your business, not the other way around.
* **Relationships matter** — Every record exists within a larger context.
* **Minimal complexity** — Powerful without becoming overwhelming.
* **Developer-friendly** — Built with modern technologies and a strong focus on maintainability.

## Planned Features

* Custom workflows
* Custom fields
* Record relationships
* Dynamic forms
* Automation engine
* Activity timeline
* File management
* Search and filtering
* Role-based permissions
* REST API
* Webhooks

## Philosophy

Software should adapt to people.

Eolian aims to become a platform where workflows feel natural, data stays connected, and every interaction contributes to a clearer picture of how a team works.

## Project Status

> 🚧 Early development.

The project is currently under active development and the architecture is expected to evolve as new ideas are explored.
